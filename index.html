Biological Drivers

Why Hair Fall Happens

Hair loss is rarely just superficial. It is a biological signal of internal, hormonal, genetic, or micro-environmental distress. Effective treatment requires pinpointing your exact root causes.

Genetics & DHT Sensitivity

Inherited sensitivity to dihydrotestosterone causes progressive follicle miniaturization, resulting in shorter growth cycles and thinning.

Clinical diagnostic indicator

Chronic Stress & Cortisol

Prolonged mental or physical stress triggers early follicle transition from anagen (growth) into telogen (shedding) effluvium.

Clinical diagnostic indicator

Nutritional Deficiencies

Low serum ferritin, vitamin D3, vitamin B12, or amino acids starve the follicular matrix of essential cellular building blocks.

Clinical diagnostic indicator
