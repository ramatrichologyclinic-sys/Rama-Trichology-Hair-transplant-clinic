ROOT CAUSES

Why Hair Fall Happens — Hair health starts from within

Hair loss is rarely just superficial. It is an internal biological signal of hormonal, nutritional, genetic, or digestive distress. Effective recovery requires pinpointing your root causes.

Nutrition

A diet low in iron, biotin, or essential proteins deprives the hair matrix and slows follicular regeneration.

Dandruff

Persistent flaking, sebum imbalance, and scalp inflammation weaken follicle anchor roots and accelerate shedding.

Gut Issues

Poor microbiome health, chronic acidity, or impaired digestion blocks critical mineral absorption to hair roots.

Stress

Elevated cortisol pushes active growing follicles prematurely into the telogen resting phase, causing sudden shedding.

Family History

When hair fall runs in your family, genetic sensitivity causes progressive follicle miniaturization over time.

Metabolism

Sluggish cellular metabolism and nutrient transport leave hair strands dry, weak, brittle, and prone to breakage.

System Diseases

Thyroid irregularities, chronic anemia, insulin resistance, or autoimmune conditions directly disrupt follicle cycling.

Few Medications

Certain prescription therapies, blood pressure medications, or hormonal treatments can trigger sudden telogen shedding.

Environmental Conditions

Hard mineral water, extreme urban pollution, seasonal shifts, and intense UV rays degrade hair cuticles and stress the scalp.
