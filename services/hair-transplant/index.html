Permanent Follicular Unit Relocation

What is Modern Follicular Unit Extraction?

A modern hair transplant is an intricate microsurgical procedure where genetically permanent, DHT-resistant follicles are harvested individually from the occipital safe donor area and meticulously implanted into recipient thinning zones.

Under the clinical oversight of Dr. Ritesh Safariya, we emphasize natural artistic hairline design matching your facial symmetry, ensuring optimal graft survival, zero over-harvesting, and invisible scar-free healing.

Transplant Indications

  • Advanced male pattern baldness (Norwood Stages III – VII)
  • Female pattern hair thinning unresponsive to medical therapy
  • Traction alopecia or traumatic hairline scarring
  • Receded temporal peaks and thinning crown vertex

Candidacy Criteria

  • Adequate donor density in the back and sides of the scalp
  • Stabilized active hair loss through prior medical management
  • Realistic expectations regarding age-appropriate density
  • Absence of active diffuse alopecia areata or lichen planopilaris
